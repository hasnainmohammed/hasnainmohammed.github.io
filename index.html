About me

Engineering at the intersection of
sky & silicon

I'm a Year 3 Aerospace Engineering student at HKUST, currently on exchange at NUS Singapore. I'm passionate about the full stack of flight — from structural mechanics and aerodynamics to the autonomous systems that make modern aircraft intelligent.

My minors in Robotics and Information Technology let me bridge the physical and digital: I design structures, write firmware, run CFD and FEA simulations, and build machine learning models that solve real engineering problems.
