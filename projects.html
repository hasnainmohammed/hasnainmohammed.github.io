Aerospace CAD / FEA 2024–25

Astrix Rocketry — Structures Team Lead

As Structures Team Lead at HKUST's Astrix Rocketry and Satellite Club, I was responsible for the structural design, simulation, and integrity of rocket components. This involved material selection, FEA analysis using SolidWorks Simulation, and coordinating fabrication through CNC machining, laser cutting, and 3D printing.

Key challenges included optimising the mass-to-strength ratio of fin brackets and body tube joints under predicted aerodynamic loads, while ensuring manufacturability with available workshop tools.

  • Led a subteam of 4 members
  • Performed FEA on critical load-bearing joints
  • Material selection for composite and aluminium structures
  • Coordinated fabrication and assembly

ML Aerospace Research 2025

ML Methods for Airfoil Aerodynamic Optimisation

Research internship at Temasek Laboratories, NUS, under Professor Nasr Mahmoud. Conducted a comprehensive literature review of neural network and surrogate machine learning methods applied to airfoil aerodynamic coefficient prediction and shape optimisation.

The review was formatted to MDPI Aerospace journal standards in LaTeX/Overleaf, covering physics-informed neural networks, Gaussian process regression, and gradient-based surrogate models. Findings were presented to the research group with a full slide deck and spoken script.

  • Surveyed 30+ papers on ML-driven aerodynamic optimisation
  • Compared PINN, GPR, and classical surrogate approaches
  • Produced MDPI-formatted LaTeX literature review
  • Delivered research presentation to lab group

Robotics Embedded Systems 2024

STM32 Rescue Operation — Embedded Firmware

Developed embedded firmware for a "RESCUE OPERATION" system on the STM32L4S5 microcontroller as part of EE2028 at NUS. The system implemented a dual-mode state machine (RESCUE and BATTLE modes) controlled via UART commands and sensor inputs.

Features included I2C gyroscope integration with threshold-based warnings, PWM buzzer feedback, and comprehensive state transitions documented with flowcharts and inline code comments.

  • Dual-mode state machine (RESCUE / BATTLE)
  • UART command parsing and I2C sensor integration
  • Gyroscope threshold alerts with buzzer feedback
  • Fully documented firmware with flowcharts
STM32L4S5 C UART / I2C ARM Cortex-M4

Robotics Aerospace 2024

CanSat Camera FOV System

Designed a 45°-angled camera system for a CanSat competition, computing ground coverage footprints and optimising the field-of-view geometry for low-altitude deployment. The analysis included geometric modelling of the projected elliptical ground footprint and optimisation of camera tilt angle for maximum usable coverage area.

  • Geometric FOV analysis for angled camera deployment
  • Ground footprint computation at varying altitudes
  • Camera tilt optimisation for coverage maximisation
